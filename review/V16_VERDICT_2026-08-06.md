**2. Per-clip subject quality varies a LOT within one render.** The alien's head
in the SECOND field-scene clip is "MUCH better" than in the FIRST. Both clips
used the same scene anchor image and the same prompt, so this is take variance
that `--judge-select` did not catch: the judge scores articulation/sync
(mouth-state transitions, open fraction, sync contrast) and has NO notion of
head/face fidelity, so it can happily select a well-synced take with a degraded
head. This is a real, specific gap in the judge, and Andrew found it by eye in
the first cut where two clips shared a scene -- exactly the comparison that
makes it visible.

silently reintroducing the A/V drift that got segmented trim+concat BANNED.
`features/song_video/dof_finish.py` in V2 is an explicit NOT-IMPLEMENTED stub
